Cancellation & Refund Policy

We want you to love your purchase from Petcaart. If something isn’t right, we’re here to help.

Order Cancellation

  • Cancel within 12 hours of placing the order by contacting support@petcaart.com or +91 9501684143.
  • Orders already shipped cannot be cancelled.

Returns & Replacements

  • Eligible within 7 days of delivery if the product is damaged/defective or the wrong item is received.
  • Items must be unused, in original condition, with tags, manuals, and packaging intact.
  • Provide order ID and photos of the issue to speed up resolution.

Non-returnable Items

  • Personalized/engraved items.
  • Hygiene-sensitive items (e.g., grooming tools once unsealed).
  • Perishable consumables (e.g., treats/food) if opened or partially used.

Refund Method & Timeline

  • After inspection/approval, refunds are initiated to the original payment method.
  • Processing time: 7–10 business days (bank/gateway dependent).

How to Raise a Request

  1. Email support@petcaart.com with order ID and issue details, or call +91 9501684143.
  2. Our team will share pickup/return instructions where applicable.
Note: If the returned item fails quality checks (used, damaged, or missing parts), the refund may be reduced or rejected.
